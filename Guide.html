Welcome to Contour.

A native DJ workspace for your Mac and DIY USB MIDI controller.

Start a mix

Two synthesized demo tracks are loaded and paused on launch. Press Play to hear them. Import your audio with Command–O, or drop files onto a deck or the library. Select a library track and use Load Selected, or double-click it to load into the active deck. Pause a deck before replacing its track.

Your decks

Click or drag the waveform to seek. While paused, Cue stores the current position; while playing it stops and returns to the stored cue. Click an empty Hotcue to save it, and click again to recall it. Right-click a Hotcue to clear it. Cues and edited source BPM save locally.

Choose a beat length and turn on the loop. The backward and forward buttons jump that many beats. BPM is estimated from audio: click the BPM number to correct it. Loops assume constant tempo and a beat grid beginning at time zero. Match adjusts playback rate to the other deck’s BPM; it does not align beat phase. Speed changes pitch in this build.

Mix and record

Each channel has high, mid and low EQ, a low/high-pass filter and volume. The equal-power crossfader groups A/C on the left and B/D on the right. Master level is in Settings. Echo, Space and Flanger use delay-based processing. Rhythm adds a synthesized drum pattern. Record captures the master mix to the WAV file you choose; stop recording before opening the completed file.

Connect your controller

The included profile comes from your YaSushi firmware. The mixer’s 13 analog controls combine paired CC values for 14-bit resolution. Notes and relative encoders control transport, browsing and jogs. The separate AS5600 jog module uses channels 1 and 2 for A and B, CC32 movement, Note64 press and CC33 heartbeat.

Open MIDI Controller to see detected inputs and recent MIDI activity. Select an action, choose Learn MIDI, then move one control. Mappings save automatically. The activity display samples events at up to 20 updates per second; control actions retain their order. Jog press enables seek-style scratching; without press it nudges the track. Physical response, feedback and latency need testing with your actual hardware. Do not flash the firmware for this app.

This build

Contour 0.1.1 is an independent local development build. It does not implement Traktor’s separated Stems, Remix Sets, flexible beatgrids, beat-phase Sync, pitch-preserving time stretch, DVS, streaming services, external cue routing or broadcasting. It does not use Traktor code or models. Output follows macOS Sound settings. Original media files are referenced and never modified.
